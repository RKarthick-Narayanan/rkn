Toda and Kota: multilingualism and language vitality

Nilgiris, Tamil Nadu

Research on language maintenance and multilingual practices among Toda and Kota communities in the Nilgiris. This work investigates language choice and change in relation to social life and structural inequalities. It forms the empirical foundation of the 2025 monograph Multilinguality, Vitality, and Endangerment.

SiDHELA: endangered-language archiving

Centre for Endangered Languages, Sikkim University

Work on documenting and organising language collections within the Sikkim–Darjeeling Himalayas Endangered Language Archive (SiDHELA). The experience informed published research on the development of language archives in India and collaborative approaches to their creation.

Suvadugal: performing-arts documentation

With Alternative Media Centre and participating artists and practitioners

Suvadugal brings together documentation of performing-arts practices, artists, instruments, and associated material culture. Recent work has included documentation and 3D scanning of Silambam objects and a curated exhibition on Silambam material traditions. The initiative connects field documentation with questions of interpretation, attribution, digital representation and the continuity of artistic knowledge.

This is a collaborative initiative involving documentation and scanning teams, organisers, and community practitioners.
